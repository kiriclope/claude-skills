# /to-tickets

## What it does

Matt Pocock's skill, unchanged: it breaks the spec into tracer-bullet tickets (each the smallest piece that gives a verdict end to end), each with its blocking edges, shows you the breakdown, and posts it as sub-issues with native blocking links once you approve.

## When to reach for it

Right after /to-spec, in the same context window.

## Common questions

**What is a ticket in research?**
One arm, one analysis, one figure or one section of text, with the pre-run criterion as its acceptance criteria and the reader stories it answers.

**What if a ticket's experiment is refuted?**
It closes with its verdict. The tickets waiting on it are labeled paused and come back to you.

## It's working if

You judged granularity and blocking edges before anything was posted; the frontier shows only tickets that can really start.
