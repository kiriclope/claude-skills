# /setup-research-skills

## What it does

Configures a project once: the tracker (GitHub if the repo is private, else local .scratch/), docs/agents/ (tracker, labels, research meanings), a GLOSSARY.md you approve, the Agent skills block in CLAUDE.md, project.yaml and the skill links.

## When to reach for it

A new project, or switching trackers.

## Common questions

**Why not his setup skill?**
It writes into the same places as our project.yaml and asks about software settings; ours copies his templates and adds the research files.

## It's working if

/to-spec and /to-tickets find their tracker without asking, and sessions use the glossary's terms.
