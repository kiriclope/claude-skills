The user did not follow your last message. Say it again so someone who missed the session follows it:

1. **Where we are**, in one or two sentences: the question being worked on and what was just done.
2. **The point**, in short plain sentences, one idea each. Every run, arm, hypothesis or symbol
   named by what it is (never a bare code like M1, H2, s3, gpm); each number with what it measures,
   on which runs, and its unit.
3. **What you need from the user**, if anything, as one question with your recommended answer.

Use the project's vocabulary (its CLAUDE.md, `docs/claims.md` or glossary when present). Shorter
than the original; add context, cut detail.
