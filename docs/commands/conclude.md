# /conclude

## What it does

Runs concluding-research on one question: every card on it pooled where they can be, the status of each hypothesis, discards, contradictions, and, when there is a spec, its stories answered, open or failed.

## When to reach for it

Five or more cards on one question since its last conclusion, or before dropping or changing a direction.

## Common questions

**Does it post new tickets?**
It proposes them; /to-tickets posts them after you approve.

## It's working if

Each story has a status with its card, and the next steps are draft pre-run cards.
